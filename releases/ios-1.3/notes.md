# Calque for iPad 1.3

- Opens DWG and DXF drawings, drawn to scale: the Model page, then every layout as its own page with its viewports, scales and frozen layers
- Export with markup (⋮ menu): a PDF with your pen, text and measurements burnt in, then Share, Save to Files, Print or Open in
- Search text (🔍 at the top) with next / previous match
- Split View: "Open in New Window" in the ⋮ menu, or press and hold a recent file, to see two drawings side by side
- New icon with the folded corner
- Calque for iPad is now open source (GPL-3.0), because DWG reading uses LibreDWG

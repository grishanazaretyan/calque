# Calque for Android 1.12

- Opens DXF drawings: lines, polylines, arcs, circles, splines, text, blocks, dimensions and solid hatches, drawn to scale so measuring works straight away
- DWG files show how to save them as DXF
- Export with markup (⋮ menu): a PDF with your pen, text and measurements drawn in, then Share, Save to Files, Print or Open in
- Search text in the drawing (🔍 at the top), with next / previous match
